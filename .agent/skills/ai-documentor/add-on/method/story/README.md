## Format Story Type

Setiap story type dijelaskan dengan format:

```
**Nama Story Type**
- Kategori: ...
- Deskripsi: ...
- Key Questions: pertanyaan panduan untuk membangun narasi
- Kapan Cocok: situasi terbaik
- Typical Duration: estimasi waktu
- Contoh Opening: kalimat pembuka yang bisa langsung dipakai
- Catatan: tips tambahan
```

## 1. Transformation

> Fokus: **perubahan & pertumbuhan** — narasi yang menunjukkan transformasi dari A ke B.

### 1.1 Hero's Journey

- **Deskripsi:** Classic transformation arc — protagonist leaves ordinary world, faces trials, returns transformed. Paling universal untuk narasi perubahan.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Who is the hero?
  - What's their ordinary world?
  - What call disrupts their world?
  - What trials do they face?
  - How are they transformed?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Personal transformation stories, career journeys, product evolution narratives.
- **Typical Duration:** 20-30 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "There was a developer who spent years doing things the same way — until one day everything broke."
- **Catatan:** Struktur 12 tahap Campbell bisa disederhanakan jadi 3 act: Departure → Initiation → Return.

### 1.2 Pixar Story Spine

- **Deskripsi:** Emotional narrative structure using "once upon a time" framework. Simple tapi powerful untuk membangun tension dan resolution.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Once upon a time what?
  - Every day what happened?
  - Until one day what changed?
  - Because of that what?
  - Until finally how resolved?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Product stories, team retrospectives, pitch narratives, explanation stories.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Once upon a time, there was a system that worked perfectly — every day, without fail."
- **Catatan:** Framework ini bisa dipakai untuk storytelling apa saja — bukan hanya animasi.

### 1.3 Customer Journey

- **Deskripsi:** Narrative following customer transformation from pain point through solution to success. Fokus pada empati dan hasil.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What was the before struggle?
  - What discovery moment occurred?
  - How did they implement?
  - What transformation happened?
  - What's their new reality?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Sales narratives, case studies, product demos, user testimonials.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-25 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Six months ago, they were losing 2 hours a day to manual processes."
- **Catatan:** Fokus pada **emotional journey**, bukan hanya functional steps.

### 1.4 Challenge Overcome

- **Deskripsi:** Dramatic structure centered on confronting and conquering obstacles. High-stakes, high-engagement.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What obstacle blocked progress?
  - How did stakes escalate?
  - What was the darkest moment?
  - What breakthrough occurred?
  - What was learned?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Post-mortem stories, team achievements, problem-solving narratives, motivation.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "The deadline was 48 hours away. The system was down. And no one knew why."
- **Catatan:** Darkest moment = emotional low point. Semakin gelap, semakin kuat breakthrough-nya.

### 1.5 Character Arc

- **Deskripsi:** Personal evolution story showing growth through experience dan struggle. Fokus pada internal change.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Who are they at start?
  - What forces change?
  - What do they resist?
  - What breakthrough shifts them?
  - Who have they become?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Personal development stories, mentorship narratives, culture stories.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "She used to believe that good code was enough — until she learned what really mattered."
- **Catatan:** Character arc = internal transformation. External events hanya katalis.

## 2. Strategic

> Fokus: **visi & posisi** — narasi yang membangun arah dan memposisikan di pasar.

### 2.1 Brand Story

- **Deskripsi:** Authentic narrative communicating brand values, mission, dan unique market position. Membangun koneksi emosional.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What sparked this brand?
  - What core values drive it?
  - How does it impact customers?
  - What makes it different?
  - Where is it heading?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Company presentations, website copy, investor pitches, onboarding.
- **Typical Duration:** 20-30 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "We didn't start in a garage. We started with a frustration that wouldn't go away."
- **Catatan:** Authenticity > perfection. Cerita yang jujur lebih powerful dari cerita yang dipoles.

### 2.2 Vision Narrative

- **Deskripsi:** Future-focused story painting vivid picture of desired state dan path to get there. Inspirasi + direction.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What's the current reality?
  - What opportunity emerges?
  - What's the bold vision?
  - What's the strategic path?
  - What does transformed future look like?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Strategy presentations, team alignment, product roadmaps, keynote speeches.
- **Typical Duration:** 20-25 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Imagine a world where deployment takes seconds, not hours."
- **Catatan:** Vivid > vague. Semakin spesifik gambaran masa depan, semakin inspiring.

### 2.3 Origin Story

- **Deskripsi:** Foundational narrative explaining how something came to be dan why it matters today. Membangun meaning.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What was the spark moment?
  - What early struggles occurred?
  - What key breakthrough happened?
  - How did it evolve?
  - What's the current mission?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Company introductions, product launches, team onboarding, documentation.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "It started with a bug that nobody could reproduce."
- **Catatan:** Origin story bukan chronological history — ini **narrative** yang memilih momen-momen meaningful.

### 2.4 Positioning Story

- **Deskripsi:** Narrative establishing unique market position dan competitive differentiation. Mengapa kita berbeda.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What market gap exists?
  - How are you uniquely qualified?
  - What makes your approach different?
  - Why should audience care?
  - What future do you enable?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Sales pitches, competitive analysis, market positioning, product differentiation.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Everyone was optimizing for speed. We optimized for trust."
- **Catatan:** Positioning story harus bisa diringkas dalam 30 detik — elevator pitch version.

### 2.5 Culture Story

- **Deskripsi:** Internal narrative defining organizational values, behaviors, dan identity. Membangun shared understanding.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What principles guide decisions?
  - What behaviors exemplify culture?
  - What stories illustrate values?
  - How do people experience it?
  - What culture are you building?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Team onboarding, internal comms, culture documentation, retrospectives.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "When we had to choose between shipping fast and doing it right, here's what happened."
- **Catatan:** Culture story = concrete example, bukan abstract values. Show, don't tell.

## 3. Persuasive

> Fokus: **menggerakkan orang** — narasi yang menginspirasi tindakan.

### 3.1 Pitch Narrative

- **Deskripsi:** Compelling story structure designed to inspire action, investment, atau partnership. Problem → Solution → Proof → Ask.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What problem landscape exists?
  - What's your vision for solution?
  - What proof validates approach?
  - What's the opportunity size?
  - What action do you want?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Investor pitches, stakeholder buy-in, internal proposals, grant applications.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-25 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Every year, companies lose $X to [problem]. We found a way to fix it."
- **Catatan:** Pitch narrative harus punya **clear ask**. Tanpa call-to-action, ini cuma cerita.

### 3.2 Sales Story

- **Deskripsi:** Customer-centric narrative demonstrating value dan building desire for solution. Empathy-first.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What pain do they feel?
  - How do you understand it?
  - What solution transforms situation?
  - What results can they expect?
  - What's the path forward?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Sales conversations, product demos, proposal presentations.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "I know what it's like to spend Friday nights debugging production."
- **Catatan:** Sales story fokus pada **customer's story**, bukan product's story. Produk cuma supporting character.

### 3.3 Change Story

- **Deskripsi:** Narrative making case for transformation dan mobilizing people through transition. Address resistance.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Why can't we stay here?
  - What does better look like?
  - What's at stake if we don't?
  - How do we get there?
  - What's in it for them?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Change management, transformation initiatives, team alignment, process changes.
- **Typical Duration:** 20-25 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "We can keep doing what we're doing. But in 2 years, we'll be irrelevant."
- **Catatan:** Change story harus address the fear. Akui resistance, lalu show the path forward.

### 3.4 Fundraising Story

- **Deskripsi:** Emotionally compelling narrative connecting donor values to mission impact. Heart + hope.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What problem breaks hearts?
  - What solution creates hope?
  - What impact will investment make?
  - Why is this urgent?
  - How can they help?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Grant applications, crowdfunding, nonprofit pitches, social enterprise.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Last night, 100 people slept on the streets of our city. Here's how we change that."
- **Catatan:** Fundraising story = emotional connection + clear impact + urgent need.

### 3.5 Advocacy Story

- **Deskripsi:** Story galvanizing support for cause, movement, atau policy change. Collective action.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What injustice demands attention?
  - Who is affected and how?
  - What change is needed?
  - What happens if we act?
  - How can they join?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Policy advocacy, community organizing, awareness campaigns, social movements.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "One person can't change the system. But 10,000 people can."
- **Catatan:** Advocacy story harus punya **specific antagonist** (masalah) dan **specific protagonist** (community).

## 4. Analytical

> Fokus: **data & insight** — narasi yang mengubah fakta menjadi meaning.

### 4.1 Data Storytelling

- **Deskripsi:** Transform data insights into compelling narrative with clear actionable takeaways. Context → Insight → Action.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What context is needed?
  - What data reveals insight?
  - What patterns explain it?
  - So what, why does it matter?
  - What actions should follow?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Dashboard presentations, metric reviews, executive summaries, data reports.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-25 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "The numbers told a story nobody expected."
- **Catatan:** Data story ≠ data dump. Pilih **one insight** dan build narrative around it.

### 4.2 Case Study

- **Deskripsi:** Detailed narrative documenting real-world application, results, dan learnings. Evidence-based.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What was the situation?
  - What approach was taken?
  - What challenges emerged?
  - What results were achieved?
  - What lessons transfer?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Sales enablement, knowledge sharing, documentation, best practices.
- **Typical Duration:** 20-30 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "When [Company] tried [Approach], nobody predicted what happened next."
- **Catatan:** Case study harus punya **specific numbers** dan **specific lessons**. Generic = useless.

### 4.3 Research Narrative

- **Deskripsi:** Story structure presenting research findings in accessible engaging way. Complex → Simple.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What question drove research?
  - How was it investigated?
  - What did you discover?
  - What does it mean?
  - What are implications?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Research presentations, insight sharing, academic communication, trend analysis.
- **Typical Duration:** 20-25 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "We asked 500 developers about their biggest frustration. The answer surprised us."
- **Catatan:** Research narrative = findings + meaning + implications. Jangan methodology-heavy.

### 4.4 Insight Narrative

- **Deskripsi:** Narrative revealing non-obvious truth atau pattern that shifts understanding. AHA moment.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What did everyone assume?
  - What did you notice?
  - What deeper pattern emerged?
  - Why does it matter?
  - What should change?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Strategy presentations, insight sharing, paradigm shifts, "aha moment" stories.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Everyone thought the problem was X. We discovered it was actually Y."
- **Catatan:** Insight narrative harus punya **element of surprise**. Jika obvious, bukan insight.

### 4.5 Process Story

- **Deskripsi:** Behind-the-scenes narrative showing how something was made atau accomplished. Transparency + learning.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What was being created?
  - What approach was chosen?
  - What challenges arose?
  - How were they solved?
  - What was learned?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Retrospectives, documentation, knowledge sharing, team onboarding.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Here's exactly how we built [thing] from scratch."
- **Catatan:** Process story harus jujur tentang struggles. Polished version = tidak credible.

## 5. Emotional

> Fokus: **koneksi emosional** — narasi yang membuat orang merasa.

### 5.1 Hook Driven

- **Deskripsi:** Story structure maximizing emotional engagement through powerful opening dan touchpoints. Attention-first.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What surprising fact opens?
  - What urgent question emerges?
  - Where are emotional peaks?
  - What creates relatability?
  - What payoff satisfies?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Presentations, talks, marketing copy, social media stories.
- **Typical Duration:** 10-15 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "I have 30 seconds to save you 100 hours a year. Here's how."
- **Catatan:** First 10 seconds = everything. Hook harus unexpected atau provocative.

### 5.2 Conflict Resolution

- **Deskripsi:** Narrative centered on tension building dan satisfying resolution of core conflict. Drama arc.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What's the central conflict?
  - Who wants what and why?
  - What prevents resolution?
  - How does tension escalate?
  - How is it resolved?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Team conflict stories, product tension narratives, dramatic presentations.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Two teams. Two visions. One deadline."
- **Catatan:** Conflict harus **real** dan **relatable**. Manufactured conflict = tidak engaging.

### 5.3 Empathy Story

- **Deskripsi:** Story designed to create emotional connection dan understanding of other perspectives. Walk in their shoes.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Whose perspective are we taking?
  - What do they experience?
  - What do they feel?
  - Why should audience care?
  - What common ground exists?
- **Kapan Cocok:** User research sharing, team building, culture stories, customer understanding.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Put yourself in their shoes for a moment. You're a new developer on day one..."
- **Catatan:** Empathy story harus **specific**. Abstract experiences = tidak memorable.

### 5.4 Human Interest

- **Deskripsi:** Personal story highlighting universal human experiences dan emotions. Particular → Universal.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Who is at the center?
  - What personal stakes exist?
  - What universal themes emerge?
  - What emotional journey occurs?
  - What makes it relatable?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Team stories, culture narratives, motivation, community building.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "It was 3 AM. The build was broken. And I was alone."
- **Catatan:** Human interest = specific person, universal emotion. The more specific, the more universal.

### 5.5 Vulnerable Story

- **Deskripsi:** Authentic personal narrative sharing struggle, failure, atau raw truth to build connection. Courage + hope.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What truth is hard to share?
  - What struggle was faced?
  - What was learned?
  - Why share this now?
  - What hope does it offer?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Leadership stories, mentorship, team building, psychological safety.
- **Typical Duration:** 15-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "I failed. And it was the best thing that happened to me."
- **Catatan:** Vulnerability tanpa weakness. Ini tentang **courage**, bukan **victimhood**.

## 6. Conversational

> Fokus: **ngobrol & eksplorasi** — narasi ringan untuk percakapan sehari-hari.

### 6.1 Free Association

- **Deskripsi:** Biarkan ide melompat-lompat tanpa struktur. Dari A ke B ke Z — tanpa filter.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Apa yang terlintas di pikiran?
  - Apa koneksi antara [ide A] dan [ide B]?
  - Ke mana ini membawa kita?
  - Apa yang mengejutkan?
  - Apa yang bisa dieksplorasi?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Ngobrol santai, eksplorasi ide, warm-up session, creative conversation.
- **Typical Duration:** 10-20 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Kalau kamu bisa punya satu superpower teknologi, apa?"
- **Catatan:** Tidak ada jawaban yang salah. Tujuannya adalah **exploration**, bukan **conclusion**.

### 6.2 Thought Experiment

- **Deskripsi:** "What if..." tanpa harus solve sesuatu. Eksplorasi murni untuk kesenangan atau insight.
- **Key Questions:**
  - What if [scenario absurd]?
  - Apa yang akan terjadi?
  - Apa implikasinya?
  - Apa yang kita pelajari dari eksperimen ini?
  - Bagaimana ini mengubah perspektif kita?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Ngobrol santai, philosophical discussion, perspective exploration.
- **Typical Duration:** 10-15 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "What if semua bug di dunia hilang besok — apa yang terjadi?"
- **Catatan:** Tidak perlu jawaban "benar". Proses berpikirnya yang penting.

### 6.3 Analogi & Metaphor

- **Deskripsi:** Jelaskan konsep lewat perbandingan sehari-hari. Abstract → Concrete.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Ini mirip dengan apa?
  - Bagaimana metafora ini bekerja?
  - Apa yang terungkap oleh analogi ini?
  - Di mana analoginya tidak cocok?
  - Apa insight dari perbandingan ini?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Penjelasan teknis ke non-teknis, ngobrol santai, teaching moments.
- **Typical Duration:** 10-15 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Microservices itu seperti orkestra — setiap musisi bermain sendiri, tapi harus harmonis."
- **Catatan:** Analogi yang baik mengungkap insight. Analogi yang buruk cuma bikin bingung.

### 6.4 Devil's Advocate Ringan

- **Deskripsi:** Tantang asumsi cara santai — bukan debat, tapi perspective shifting.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Apa kalau yang sebaliknya benar?
  - Apa yang terlewat dari pandangan ini?
  - Siapa yang tidak setuju dan kenapa?
  - Apa yang bisa kita pelajari dari sudut pandang berlawanan?
  - Apa middle ground-nya?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Ngobrol santai, assumption checking, perspective broadening.
- **Typical Duration:** 10-15 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Devil's advocate ya — bagaimana kalau microservices itu sebenarnya tidak worth it?"
- **Catatan:** Tetap playful. Tujuannya bukan menang, tapi **explore**.

### 6.5 Reflective Listening

- **Deskripsi:** Paraphrase untuk memastikan paham dan deepen understanding. Listening-first.
- **Key Questions:**
  - Apa yang kamu dengar?
  - Apa yang tersirat tapi tidak dikatakan?
  - Apa yang paling penting dari cerita ini?
  - Apa yang membuat cerita ini meaningful?
  - Apa yang ingin kamu eksplorasi lebih?
- **Kapan Cocok:** Ngobrol santai, mentoring, active listening practice.
- **Typical Duration:** 10-15 menit
- **Contoh Opening:** "Jika saya memahami dengan benar, kamu bilang..."
- **Catatan:** Reflective listening = **understanding**, bukan **responding**. Dengar dulu, baru respons.

## Matriks Pemilihan

### Berdasarkan Tujuan Komunikasi

| Tujuan | Story Type Rekomendasi |
|---|---|
| **Inspirasi / motivasi** | Hero's Journey, Human Interest, Vulnerable Story |
| **Jelaskan konsep** | Analogi & Metaphor, Process Story, Pixar Spine |
| **Persuasi / jual** | Pitch Narrative, Sales Story, Customer Journey |
| **Bangun empati** | Empathy Story, Human Interest, Customer Journey |
| **Presentasi data** | Data Storytelling, Insight Narrative, Case Study |
| **Change management** | Change Story, Challenge Overcome, Vision Narrative |
| **Ngobrol santai** | Free Association, Thought Experiment, Devil's Advocate |
| **Dokumentasi** | Process Story, Case Study, Research Narrative |
| **Team building** | Culture Story, Vulnerable Story, Human Interest |
| **Strategic alignment** | Vision Narrative, Brand Story, Positioning Story |

### Berdasarkan Energi Sesi

| Energi | Story Type |
|---|---|
| **Tinggi** | Hero's Journey, Challenge Overcome, Pitch Narrative |
| **Sedang** | Most story types |
| **Rendah** | Reflective Listening, Free Association, Analogi |

### Berdasarkan Durasi

| Waktu | Story Type |
|---|---|
| **< 10 menit** | Analogi, Free Association, Hook Driven |
| **10-20 menit** | Most single story types |
| **20-30 menit** | Hero's Journey, Case Study, Vision Narrative |
| **Sesi panjang** | Multiple story types in sequence |

### Berdasarkan Persona

| Persona | Story Type yang Cocok |
|---|---|
| 🔬 **Problem Solver** | Challenge Overcome, Data Storytelling, Insight Narrative, Process Story |
| ✨ **Creative Visionary** | Hero's Journey, Pixar Spine, Empathy Story, Thought Experiment |
| 🏗️ **Technical Architect** | Vision Narrative, Process Story, Case Study, Analogi |
| 📊 **Data Specialist** | Data Storytelling, Insight Narrative, Research Story, Case Study |
| ⚙️ **Backend Engineer** | Process Story, Challenge Overcome, Case Study, Human Interest |
